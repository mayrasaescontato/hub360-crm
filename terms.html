Sam Sushi Art Music - SMS Program
Last updated: July 2026

Program Description

Sam Sushi Art Music offers an SMS program to send order updates, reservation confirmations, and customer service messages to customers who have provided their phone number.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us, such as placing an order or making a reservation.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details on your messaging plan.

Opt-In and Opt-Out

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Sam Sushi Art Music. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.

Support

For questions about this SMS program, please contact Sam Sushi Art Music directly.
