Privacy Policy

Sam Sushi Art Music
Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sam Sushi Art Music ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you interact with our restaurant, including by phone, SMS, or in person.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, and order or reservation details when you contact us or place an order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to confirm reservations, provide order updates, respond to customer service requests, and communicate with you about your experience with us. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from Sam Sushi Art Music related to order updates, reservation confirmations, and customer service. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us directly.

Data Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or mobile network is completely secure.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Sam Sushi Art Music directly.
